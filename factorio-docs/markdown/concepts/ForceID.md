# ForceID

_concept_

A force may be specified in one of three ways.

**Definition:** [string](../concepts/string.md) — Name of the force, same as [LuaForce::name](../classes/LuaForce.md#name). ∣ [uint8](../concepts/uint8.md) — Index of the force, same as [LuaForce::index](../classes/LuaForce.md#index). ∣ [LuaForce](../classes/LuaForce.md) — A reference to [LuaForce](../classes/LuaForce.md) may be passed directly.

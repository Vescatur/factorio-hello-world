# PlayerIdentification

_concept_

A player may be specified in one of three ways.

**Definition:** [uint32](../concepts/uint32.md) — The player index. ∣ [string](../concepts/string.md) — The player name. ∣ [LuaPlayer](../classes/LuaPlayer.md) — A reference to [LuaPlayer](../classes/LuaPlayer.md) may be passed directly.

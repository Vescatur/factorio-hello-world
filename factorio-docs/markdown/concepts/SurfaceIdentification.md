# SurfaceIdentification

_concept_

A surface may be specified in one of three ways.

**Definition:** [uint32](../concepts/uint32.md) — It will be the index of the surface. `nauvis` has index `1`, the first surface-created surface will have index `2` and so on. ∣ [string](../concepts/string.md) — It will be the surface name. E.g. `"nauvis"`. ∣ [LuaSurface](../classes/LuaSurface.md) — A reference to [LuaSurface](../classes/LuaSurface.md) may be passed directly.
